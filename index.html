01 / ABOUT

Technology, security
and national purpose.

My work sits where cybersecurity engineering, artificial intelligence and defence meet. I combine operational and professional experience in cyber defence with research into how emerging technologies can be evaluated, secured and deployed responsibly in high-consequence environments.

I completed postgraduate study in Cyber Security Engineering at the University of Warwick as a Chevening Scholar. My current interests include AI security and assurance, threat intelligence, attack-graph generation, federated identity security, cryptography and evidence-led adoption of AI-enabled cyber capabilities.

02 / FEATURED RESEARCH

ATTACK GRAPHS · FEDERATED IDENTITY · AI/ML

FIMSGraph

Domain-Constrained Attack-Graph Generation for Federated Identity Management Systems

Research into synthetic attack-graph generation for complex federated identity environments, examining how model architecture and domain-specific encoding affect graph-generation accuracy and responsiveness to threat intelligence.

The work develops a reproducible benchmark grounded in domain constraints and evaluates machine-learning and language-model approaches against logically validated scenarios.
